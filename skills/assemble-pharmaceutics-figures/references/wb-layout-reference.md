# Western blot reference layout profile

Use this profile for WB strip groups paired with densitometry charts. It was measured from a read-only `170 × 200 mm` Illustrator reference and must be mapped onto the project's default `A4 portrait` working canvas rather than copied at its original size.

## Structure

- Preserve a two-column, three-level reading grid: top WB strips, middle quantification, bottom quantification or secondary endpoints.
- Share vertical centerlines within each column. Match the visible plotting width of corresponding left/right charts.
- Keep panel labels at a common top-left offset and retain alphabetical reading order.
- Pair each WB strip group with its matching quantification; do not infer scientific identities from this reference.

## Spacing hierarchy

| Relationship | Starting value on A4 portrait |
|---|---:|
| `intra-strip` gap | 1.5–2 mm |
| Strip to its label | 1–1.5 mm |
| Elements inside one WB group | 2–3 mm |
| Left/right column gap | 8 mm |
| WB level to quantification level | 7–9 mm |
| Adjacent top-level panels | 6–8 mm |

Use an intra-strip : inter-panel ratio near `2:7`. Scale values proportionally when the effective figure width changes, but keep the hierarchy.

## A4 portrait mapping

- Keep the working canvas at 210 × 297 mm.
- Start with 15 mm left/right safety margins, 180 mm effective width, an 8 mm gutter, and two columns of about 86 mm.
- Map the reference's normalized figure block to roughly 180 × 212 mm and center it. Do not stretch panels vertically merely to fill the unused A4 height.
- Size strips and charts from approved visible-content bounds, not from raster canvases containing white margins.

## Integrity limits

- Treat blue/black boxes, labels, molecular-weight text, and panel letters as layout examples only.
- Do not copy treatment names, targets, molecular weights, significance marks, or conclusions into another project.
- Do not crop or rearrange WB lanes without explicit user approval and traceability.
